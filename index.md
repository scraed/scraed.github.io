## About Me

I am currently a postdoctoral fellow in the [Department of Mathematics](https://www.math.hkust.edu.hk/) at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from HKUST, previously studied in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at HKUST, and earned my B.S. in Physics from SUSTech. My work sits at the intersection of generative modeling, machine learning, and physics.


## Research Interests

My research focuses on two main directions:

- **Generative Diffusion Models**: I study mathematically and physically motivated methods for more controllable diffusion-based generation, with interests in image editing, reference-based generation, image restoration, inpainting, and guidance. Related open-source projects include [LanPaint](https://github.com/scraed/LanPaint) and [Characteristic Guidance Web UI](https://github.com/scraed/CharacteristicGuidanceWebUI).
- **Non-equilibrium Physics**: I work on particle kinetics and gas dynamics governed by the Fokker-Planck equation and the Boltzmann equation, including applications of AI to shock waves, light scattering, and constitutive relations.

### Moment Gauge
Try our python & JAX library [Moment Gauge](https://github.com/scraed/TheMomentGauge), designed to facilitate the implementation of numerical solvers using the maximal entropy moment method. Built on the JAX framework, Moment Gauge aims to provide reusable code for researchers and developers working with rarefied gas dynamics and other applications of the maximal entropy moment method.
